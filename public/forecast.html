A first-cut model: each GXP's distributed solar is treated as one north-facing array tilted 20°. Irradiance is from Himawari satellite readings around the GXP for times that have passed (about half an hour behind), and the 15-minute forecast after that. Capacities are registry nameplate MW AC, net of dispatched solar farms. Data from the NZ Solar Stats API.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0); satellite data from JMA/JAXA Himawari.
